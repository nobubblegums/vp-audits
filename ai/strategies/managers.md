## Decision or contract question

How do managers create state and pressure consumed by later decisions?

Economic, military, diplomacy, grand-strategy and specialization outputs can be flavor mutations or direct state reads. Only represented decision contracts are documented; this is not an exhaustive audit of every manager decision.

## Trigger and ownership

`CvPlayer::doTurnPostDiplomacy` in `CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvPlayer.cpp` is a central player-turn hub. It updates threat, plots, danger, military stats, economic AI, military AI, and for major civs religion/trade/city-specialization/league AI before city turns. It then calls `AI_doSpaceshipProduction`, loops cities through `CvCity::doTurn`, resolves policy AI, research, espionage, religion player turn processing through `CvGameReligions::DoPlayerTurn`, leagues, trade, events, and yield history.

## Scoring and inputs

- this is the flavor infrastructure itself. Important APIs are `GetPersonalityIndividualFlavor`, `GetLatestFlavorValue`, and `CvGrandStrategyAI::GetPersonalityAndGrandStrategy`.

The player-turn ordering that makes these managers upstream of city production is `CvPlayer::doTurn()` -> `CvGrandStrategyAI::DoTurn()` and `CvDiplomacyAI::DoTurn(...)`, then `CvPlayer::doTurnPostDiplomacy()` -> `CvEconomicAI::DoTurn()`, `CvMilitaryAI::DoTurn()`, and for major civs `CvCitySpecializationAI::DoTurn()` before the city loop (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvPlayer.cpp`, functions `CvPlayer::doTurn`, `CvPlayer::doTurnPostDiplomacy`).

These managers produce two broad kinds of downstream state:

- Flavor deltas: `CvEconomicAI::DoTurn()` and `CvMilitaryAI::UpdateMilitaryStrategies()` apply `AIEconomicStrategy_Player_Flavors`, `AIEconomicStrategy_City_Flavors`, `AIMilitaryStrategy_Player_Flavors`, and `AIMilitaryStrategy_City_Flavors` through `CvFlavorManager::ChangeActivePersonalityFlavors(...)` and `CvFlavorManager::ChangeCityFlavors(...)` (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvEconomicAI.cpp`, `CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvMilitaryAI.cpp`). New cities receive active economic/military city deltas in `CvCityStrategyAI::UpdateFlavorsForNewCity()` (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvCityStrategyAI.cpp`).

- Direct state: `CvGrandStrategyAI` exposes active grand strategy, grand-strategy priorities, and `GetPersonalityAndGrandStrategy(...)`; `CvDiplomacyAI` exposes victory pursuit, closeness-to-victory, war/approach/opinion/dispute/block state; `CvMilitaryAI` exposes recommended army/navy/explorer counts, war type, defense state, operation demand, and skip counters; `CvCitySpecializationAI` exposes desired wonder and city specialization assignment state.

RISK: The same concept can appear as both a flavor and a direct strategy check. Examples include conquest/offense (`FLAVOR_OFFENSE`, `AIGRANDSTRATEGY_CONQUEST`, diplomacy world-conquest pursuit, military war strategies), diplomacy (`FLAVOR_DIPLOMACY`, diplomacy victory pursuit, city-state strategies), wonder (`FLAVOR_WONDER`, desired wonder, best-wonder city, rival wonder construction), and naval pressure (`FLAVOR_NAVAL`, recommended navy size, naval strategies, coastal specialization).

`CvEconomicAI::DoTurn()` updates economic monitoring, recon state (`DoReconState()`), antiquity-site state (`DoAntiquitySites()`), then loops `CvEconomicAIStrategyXMLEntry` rows and starts/ends `EconomicAIStrategyTypes` based on hardcoded trigger functions plus the Lua hook `EconomicStrategyCanActivate` (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvEconomicAI.cpp`, `CvEconomicAI::DoTurn`).

Economic strategy rows load flavor deltas from database tables `AIEconomicStrategy_Player_Flavors`, `AIEconomicStrategy_City_Flavors`, and threshold modifiers from `AIEconomicStrategy_PersonalityFlavorThresholdMods` through `CvEconomicAIStrategyXMLEntry::CacheResults`, `GetPlayerFlavorValue(int)`, and `GetCityFlavorValue(int)` (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvEconomicAI.cpp`). Source-data changes appear in `(1) Community Patch/Database Changes/AI/CoreStrategyChanges.sql`, `(2) Vox Populi/Database Changes/AI/StrategyChanges.sql`, and `(2) Vox Populi/Database Changes/AI/StrategyFlavorSweeps.sql`.

State produced includes active economic strategies (`CvEconomicAI::IsUsingStrategy(EconomicAIStrategyTypes)`), recon/naval recon state (`GetReconState()`, `GetNavalReconState()`), explorer demand (`m_iExplorersNeeded`, `m_iNavalExplorersNeeded` and accessors), visible antiquity-site counters, explorer/worker disband counters, and purchase/hurry demand (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvEconomicAI.h`).

Downstream consumers include city and unit production. `CvCityStrategyAI::ChooseProduction(...)` checks `ECONOMICAISTRATEGY_LOSING_MONEY` before adding maintenance buildings; `CvUnitProductionAI::CheckUnitBuildSanity(...)` checks economic strategies such as `ECONOMICAISTRATEGY_ENOUGH_EXPANSION`, `ECONOMICAISTRATEGY_NEED_DIPLOMATS`, `ECONOMICAISTRATEGY_NEED_DIPLOMATS_CRITICAL`, and `ECONOMICAISTRATEGY_GS_SPACESHIP` (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvCityStrategyAI.cpp`, `CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvUnitProductionAI.cpp`).

Policy scoring consumes economic strategy state directly: `CvPlayerPolicies::AddFlavorAsStrategies()` boosts `FLAVOR_GOLD` when `ECONOMICAISTRATEGY_LOSING_MONEY` is active (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvPolicyClasses.cpp`). Religion scoring also has direct economic strategy checks for reformation-era belief scoring, including `ECONOMICAISTRATEGY_NEED_HAPPINESS` and `ECONOMICAISTRATEGY_NEED_HAPPINESS_CRITICAL` (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvReligionClasses.cpp`, `CvReligionAI::ScoreBeliefForPlayer` area).

State produced includes active military strategies (`CvMilitaryAI::IsUsingStrategy`), land/naval defense states (`GetLandDefenseState()`, `GetNavalDefenseState()`), recommended military sizes (`GetRecommendedMilitarySize()`, `GetRecommendLandArmySize()`, `GetRecommendNavySize()`, `GetRecommendedExplorers()`), role counters (`m_iNumRangedLandUnits`, `m_iNumAntiAirUnits`, `m_iNumArcherLandUnits`, etc.), attack targets (`CvAttackTarget`), operation demand, and production skip counters (`GetNumberOfTimesOpsBuildSkippedOver()`, `GetNumberOfTimesSettlerBuildSkippedOver()`) (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvMilitaryAI.h`).

Unit production consumes this state heavily. `CvUnitProductionAI::CheckUnitBuildSanity(...)` checks recommended land/naval/explorer counts, war state, war type, naval need strategies, barbarian strategies, role need/enough strategies, anti-air data, operation skip pressure, and settler skip pressure (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvUnitProductionAI.cpp`). `CvCityStrategyAI::ChooseProduction(...)` injects `CITY_BUILDABLE_UNIT_FOR_OPERATION` using `CvCity::GetUnitForOperation()` and `CITY_BUILDABLE_UNIT_FOR_ARMY` using `CvMilitaryAI::GetUnitTypeForArmy(CvCity*)`, then weights those with `FLAVOR_OFFENSE` from grand strategy and military skip counters (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvCityStrategyAI.cpp`).

Building/wonder scoring consumes military state directly. `CvBuildingProductionAI::CheckBuildingBuildSanity(...)` uses recommended military size, number of civs at war, and war-state checks (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvBuildingProductionAI.cpp`). `CvCitySpecializationAI::WeightProductionSubtypes()` uses active military strategies and number of civs at war to weight production specializations (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvCitySpecializationAI.cpp`).

State produced includes active grand strategy (`GetActiveGrandStrategy()`), turns since active set, per-grand-strategy priorities (`GetGrandStrategyPriority(...)`), guessed other-player strategies/confidence, and cached flavor accessors in `CvGrandStrategyAI.h`. `GetPersonalityAndGrandStrategy(FlavorTypes, bool)` returns base personality flavor plus active grand strategy flavor mod, optionally blended toward the active grand strategy's main flavor (`CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvGrandStrategyAI.cpp`).

Downstream consumers include policy scoring and ideology choice (`CvPlayerPolicies::AddFlavorAsStrategies`, `CvPolicyAI::DoChooseIdeology`, `CvPolicyAI::ChooseNextPolicy`), religion scoring (`CvReligionAI::ScoreBeliefForPlayer`), desired wonder/city specialization (`CvCitySpecializationAI::WeightSpecializations`, `WeightProductionSubtypes`), unit operation weights (`CvCityStrategyAI::ChooseProduction`), and military/economic helper tests (`CvMilitaryAI.cpp`, `CvEconomicAI.cpp`).

INFERRED: Grand strategy is not a normal flavor recipient. It mostly produces direct query state and a modified flavor accessor; it does not itself call `CvFlavorManager::ChangeActivePersonalityFlavors(...)` when the active strategy changes.

RISK: `GetPersonalityAndGrandStrategy(...)` is a high-risk shared concept because many systems use it instead of recipient latest flavors. Changing its scale or active-strategy blend affects policies, ideology, specialization, religion, production, military composition tests, tech valuation, and diplomacy-adjacent scoring.
