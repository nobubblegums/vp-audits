How Vox Populi’s AI scores and selects units for city production

This guide summarizes the source-audited VP 5.4.8 production logic. The central path is: build an initial unit weight from city flavors, run the unit sanity checks, adjust surviving scores for production time, then choose from the final city-production list. Units compete in the same city-production system as buildings, projects, and processes.

1. From city flavors to an initial unit weight

When city production flavors are rebuilt, each unit gets a cached flavor weight:

City flavor input
effectiveFlavor[f] = max(0, cityFlavor[f])
Per-flavor contribution
unitFlavorCoeff[u,f] × effectiveFlavor[f]
Cached unit flavor weight
weight[u] = Σ contributions
Ordinary admission
only weight[u] > 0 enters the raw production list

There is no averaging or normalization at this stage. Square-root compression happens later, inside the sanity scorer. Operation and army candidates use different initial-weight formulas described below.

2. City-production pipeline

1
Build the raw candidate list
Add legal operation requests, army requests, ordinary units with positive initial weight, and the eligible buildings, projects, and processes.
2
Sort by initial weight
The raw list is stable-sorted from highest to lowest before sanity scoring. This order also matters for later tie behavior.
3
Run sanity checks
Every unit candidate is tested in source order. Hard failures remove it. Surviving units start from int(sqrt(abs(10 × InitialWeight))) and receive the ordered bonuses and penalties listed in section 3.
4
Adjust for production time
Each surviving score is reduced according to turns left: FinalWeight = int(SanityWeight / T^(0.15 + 0.015×T)) using the native float/double evaluation order.
5
Fallback only if nobody survived
If the post-sanity list is completely empty, the AI restores the original raw list. That fallback uses the original weights and skips both sanity and duration adjustment.
6
Check deterministic winners
Before any weighted draw, the AI may continue current production, pick a valid victory project, or choose a top defense process.
7
Apply cutoff and weighted draw
Otherwise, candidates at or above the difficulty cutoff compete proportionally to their final weights. The default cutoff in the audited reference DB is 90% of the best score.
8
Submit the winner to the queue
pushOrder clears/rechecks the queue state and may still reject the selected candidate. Selection and successful queue application are separate events.
Do not conflate three things: highest final score, membership in the cutoff-eligible pool, and the actual selected winner.

3. Where the normal path is bypassed or short-circuited

Not every production decision reaches the final weighted draw. Some paths bypass the common contest entirely; others enter it but stop before random selection.

Wrapper protections / direct controllersExisting spaceship/Protocol production can be protected, and the desired-wonder/Engineer path or player-level space/Protocol controller can assign production without the shared city contest.
Role recommendationRecommendUnit is a separate deterministic preselection for a requested UnitAI role: legal role members are duration-adjusted and the highest is returned. The resulting operation/army candidate can then enter the shared contest.
Raw-list fallbackIf every post-sanity candidate is gone, the city restores the original sorted raw list. That fallback bypasses both sanity results and duration adjustment.

5. Special candidate paths

Ordinary units

Use the cached flavor weight as InitialWeight, then run normal sanity scoring and duration reweighting.

Operation request

InitialWeight = 5000 + (OFFENSE + skippedOps) × 250 + unit cached flavor weight. The skipped-operation counter is incremented before sanity, and a surviving special candidate is ×3 during early expansion.

Army request

InitialWeight = 750 + OFFENSE × 250. It uses the same operation-context sanity path later, including the verified muster-slot requirement.

Settlers

Expansion score is built from GS Expansion, settle quality, early-expansion +100, feeder +50, WLTKD +10, unhappiness −10, capital adjustments, and skipped-settler pressure; then ×23 is added to the bonus accumulator.

Workers / workboats

Workers use city improvement demand and WANT/NEED strategy multipliers. Workboats use safe reachable plots and net local resource-improvement demand.

Air units

Bombers and fighters use a 2:3 balance test; accepted fighter/bomber candidates get 2000 + 100 × empty air slots, while other air units get +1000.

Naval units

Sea combat candidates require ocean connection, then receive navy-deficit / era / naval-need / war-domain adjustments.

Current production

Within the top half of the final ranking, the current unit can be continued deterministically before the weighted draw. Current-unit scoring also gets +100 at war, +50 at peace, or −100 when switching away from another combat unit.

6. Duration adjustment

After sanity scoring, every surviving candidate is reweighted by estimated production time:

Exponent
e = 0.15 + 0.015 × T
Divisor
d = T^e
Final weight
int(SanityWeight / d)

The exact native implementation computes the exponent using float values before widening to double for pow. A positive sanity score may truncate to 0 after this step and still remain in the final list.

7. Final selection

Ranked continuation scan

The AI scans candidates down to best / 2. The first matching current unit, protected current building, current project, or valid victory project can win immediately.

Defense-process override

If no continuation/valid-victory candidate wins, a top VP defense process with positive defense value can be chosen directly.

Cutoff pool

Otherwise: cutoff = best × percent / 100. The cutoff is inclusive. The audited reference DB uses 90 for every difficulty row.

Weighted choice

Eligible candidates are chosen proportionally to weight. If the total weight is zero, selection is uniform across the available list.

3. Exact readable pseudocode

Conventions and numeric rules

DIV and / between integers mean INTEGER DIVISION truncated toward zero, including negative values; Python // differs for negatives. Arithmetic follows native expression order. CLAMP(x,a,b) includes both endpoints. All unmarked scorer arithmetic is signed native int; no overflow guards are introduced by this specification. Out-of-range signed overflow, abs(INT_MIN), non-finite floating-to-int conversion and library boundary behavior are not assigned invented Python semantics.

F = native iFlavorWeight after compression; B = native iBonus. EARLY REJECT SR_* immediately returns that code. CONTINUE skips the indicated loop item. Role names abbreviate native UNITAI_*; strategy names carry the native prefix indicated by their owner. Default role tests differ from explicit hasRole, which tests membership in Unit_AITypes. Native input helpers remain interfaces to identified runtime/cached state, not fixed constants.

8. Initial flavor weight reconstruction

At an actual CvCityStrategyAI::FlavorUpdate callback, the four production caches are reset. Unit accumulation is:

for f in native flavor-ID order:
    effective[f] = max(0, stored_signed_city_latest[f])
    if effective[f]==0: CONTINUE
    for each valid unit u:
        contribution[u,f] = unit_coefficient[u,f] * effective[f]
        cached_weight[u] += contribution[u,f]       # starts at 0 each rebuild

There is no averaging, square root, percent divisor or normalization here. Square-root compression happens later in sanity. Effective city inputs are nonnegative, but modded coefficients can be signed; the engine does not invent positivity for the total. Generic enumeration requires total>0. A zero cache can get an unweighted coefficient-sum fallback only in RecommendUnit, not ordinary admission; a negative cache gets no such fallback. Barbarian GetWeight returns 1; RecommendUnit directly accesses the native cache instead.

City flavor storage is signed and deltas must be reversible. Clamp only the final getter, not each producer contribution. Economic/military city flavor rows, city strategies and specialization influence city latest. Active player strategy rows are routed to non-city recipients and cannot simply be added again. Single-argument GS OFFENSE/EXPANSION reads separately return personality if no active GS, otherwise max(0,personality+GS_flavorMod); the optional main-GS blending mode is false for these calls.

A specialization change and UpdateFlavorsForNewCity can change latest without rebuilding. The native cache and latest serialize separately. Therefore current active strategies or latest vectors do not prove the state that produced the consumed cache.

With current Task2U, reconstructing from AIPlayerFlavorState, AICityFlavorState and matching GameDB is conditionally possible, not guaranteed. These tables log sparse base/strategy/specialization state and transitions, not actual coefficient products or signed city vector. Replay must establish a valid initial baseline, native broadcast direction, actual per-city order, reset semantics, inherited active strategies and the cache's frozen cutoff. A player strategy can be marked active before its city broadcast; an event snapshot at that point is not proof that the new delta has already entered that particular cache. Late first observation, restored cache, or ambiguous reset/broadcast ordering can leave the exact vector unavailable.

FlavorStateID is the maximum relevant sparse event StateID frozen at actual rebuild completion, excluding direct GRAND_STRATEGY events from the player cutoff. It is not a content hash, full vector snapshot, turn number or proof of complete origins. Restored native cache has diagnostic state -1 until an observed rebuild; conflicting consumed states also produce -1. Compare a reconstructed ordinary sum to observed InitialWeight as a checksum, not proof of uniquely correct per-flavor decomposition. Current HEAD has no contribution table or COMPLETE/UNAVAILABLE reconstruction certification. Composite special seeds also need direct GS/skip/GetWeight addends and are not pure flavor sums. Compare the checksum only for source-established ordinary cache-derived seeds; the transient hasBase flag and seed origin are not persisted. GRAND_STRATEGY events are excluded from the cache cutoff and use a separate StateID axis from Decision/Event ObservationID. Thus that cutoff does not establish which direct GS was active at the exact scoring read, especially for multiple changes in one turn.

10. Final pool, cutoff and selection

The raw list is stable-sorted descending before sanity. Positive sanity survivors are appended in that order. Duration modifies scores in place, then a second stable sort preserves the earlier PRE order on ties. Consequently final ties need not follow original unit ID alone; earlier seeds/context insertion and category order matter. If POST is empty, restore sorted PRE after duration processing; if it contains zero entries, its nonempty size prevents fallback.

A sanity rejection is a return <=0, excluded before duration. A post-duration zero is a surviving candidate with zero draw weight. A PRE nonpositive cache candidate was never admitted. These are three different situations.

cutoff_weighted_draw(sorted FINAL, percent, seed):

    if FINAL[0].weight==0:

    total = sum(c.weight in source order)
    if total==0: return list[uniform_integer(0,size(list),seed)]
    r = native_integer_draw(0,total,seed)                    # WEIGHTED RANDOM DRAW
    for candidate in list order:
        r -= candidate.weight
        if r<0: return candidate

Given a uniform bounded integer draw, the intended proportional rule is P(candidate)=weight / sum(eligible weights). Native range reduction is modulo a deterministic 32-bit value, specified below; this idealized probability does not assert perfect statistical uniformity for every modulus. The cutoff is inclusive. p=100 includes all tied best candidates, not necessarily only one; p=0 gives weighted-all, not uniform unless total0. Highest0 uses uniform before testing p==0. A one-candidate pool still goes through the seeded RNG path; it does not consume a second diagnostic draw.

ChooseProduction passes CvSeeder::fromRaw(0x0e36d18b).mix(cityID). CvGame::randCore further mixes map random seed and game turn. The result is deterministic given the exact native seed context/list/weights, but it is a randomized weighted selection rule. This is not “always choose maximum,” nor an unseeded probability draw. RNG reproduction also needs native hash/mix/range arithmetic and exact ordering; current terms can report the actual draw without providing all replay context.

The complete bounded RNG arithmetic (source CvRandom.h, CvGame::randCore) is:

# Every operation in hash32/mix_raw is UNSIGNED 32-BIT; wrap modulo 2^32.
# Shifts are logical, XOR is bitwise, inputs are converted to uint first.
hash32(x):
    state = x*747796405 + 2891336453
    word = ((state >> ((state >> 28)+4)) XOR state) * 277803737
    return (word >> 22) XOR word
mix_raw(v,w):
    return v XOR (w + 0x9e3779b9 + (v << 6) + (v >> 2))
extra = mix_raw(0x0e36d18b, hash32(cityID))
random32 = mix_raw(mix_raw(mapRandomSeed, hash32(gameTurn)), extra)
bounded_draw = random32 % limit                    # limit is totalWeight or list size

Seeder-to-seeder mixing uses the contained value directly, avoiding a second hash. This path derives a value from game state; it does not advance a mutable RNG stream. Repeated calls with identical map seed/turn/city/list have the same result. “WEIGHTED RANDOM DRAW” denotes this native pseudo-random selection, not an additional source of entropy. Unsigned wrapping here is defined and differs from the unguarded signed score overflow cases.

Below-cutoff candidates and every candidate when no draw ran have -1. Uniform/highest0 and no-cutoff paths mark every source item admitted. Selected=1 means the actual pre-application winner, including deterministic continuations/overrides. It can coexist with PoolIndex=-1 and can subsequently fail pushOrder.

Operation unit candidates:

Army-fill candidates:

  • CvMilitaryAI::GetUnitTypeForArmy(CvCity* pCity) scans army open slots, tries primary then secondary slot UnitAITypes through CvUnitProductionAI::RecommendUnit(...), and prefers an army with fewer open slots (CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvMilitaryAI.cpp).
  • ChooseProduction(...) adds this as CITY_BUILDABLE_UNIT_FOR_ARMY with AI_CITYSTRATEGY_ARMY_UNIT_BASE_WEIGHT plus offense flavor multiplier (CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvCityStrategyAI.cpp).

Generic unit candidates:

  • ChooseProduction(...) loops GC.GetGameUnits()->GetNumUnits().
  • It requires m_pCity->canTrain(unit, continue-current-production).
  • Human automated cities skip most units; under MOD_BALANCE_VP, they may build noncombat units with a work rate.
  • The candidate type is CITY_BUILDABLE_UNIT, with turns from getProductionTurnsLeft(unit, 0) and initial weight from CvUnitProductionAI::GetWeight(unit).
  • Only positive-weight candidates enter m_BuildablesPrecheck.

CvUnitProductionAI::RecommendUnit(UnitAITypes eUnitAIType, bool bAllowStrategicResource) is a helper for operation/army and other purchase/religion/policy callers. It loops all units, requires CvUnitEntry::GetUnitAIType(eUnitAIType), optionally rejects units with a strategic resource type, requires CvCity::canTrain(...), falls back to sum of unit flavors if the cached unit weight is zero, reweights by turns left, stable-sorts, logs, and returns the top unit only (CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvUnitProductionAI.cpp).

  • Supply need: combat or ranged combat and not IsNoSupply().
  • Domain classification: GetDomainType() branches for DOMAIN_LAND, DOMAIN_SEA, and DOMAIN_AIR.

Scoring and inputs

Base flavor scoring:

  • CvUnitProductionAI::Reset() initializes one m_UnitAIWeights entry per unit with zero weight.
  • CvUnitProductionAI::AddFlavorWeights(FlavorTypes eFlavor, int iWeight) adds unit.GetFlavorValue(eFlavor) * iWeight.
  • CvUnitProductionAI::GetWeight(UnitTypes eUnit) returns cached weight, except barbarian cities return 1.

Sanity scoring in CvUnitProductionAI::CheckUnitBuildSanity(...):

  • Rejects nonpositive input weight as SR_IMPOSSIBLE.
  • Converts base weight to sqrti(10 * iFlavorWeight).
  • Rejects null unit entries.
  • Rejects non-free purchases that cannot be gold-purchased, and non-free non-purchase puppet city training.
  • Under siege, rejects noncombat units.
  • Rejects units in underdeveloped cities with fewer than two buildings unless desperate or purchasing.
  • Rejects units when an upgraded class can be trained somewhere.
  • Penalizes or rejects combat units near bankruptcy.
  • Rejects land/sea combat units if city garrison plus nearby placement search finds no valid plot.
  • Counts explorers, land units, and sea units including units in production; adjusts current production out of counts when not purchasing.
  • Rejects supplied land/naval/explorer units once recommended army/navy/explorer counts are reached.
  • For minor civs, applies separate era/city-count caps and recently-bullied bonuses.
  • Handles resource availability, spaceship aluminum reservation, unit investments, city-special/support/suicide units, policy-unlocked or move-after-purchase units, carriers, explorers, naval/land/air/AA demand, conquest strategy, production modifiers, diplomatic units, spaceship parts, religious combat beliefs, civilian great-person-like effects, settlers, archaeologists, work boats, workers, war boosters, best military city bonuses, promotion/unique unit bonuses, operation bonuses, underrepresented unit types, tiny army/navy pressure, siege desperation, barbarian strategy, current-production inertia, instant yield-on-completion, free-unit floor, purchase-in-siege bonus, occupied-city penalty, and final max(1, score).

Negative return codes are ProductionSkipReason in CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvCityStrategyAI.h: SR_IMPOSSIBLE, SR_UNITSUPPLY, SR_MAINTENANCE, SR_STRATEGY, SR_USELESS, and SR_BALANCE. CvCityStrategyAI::LogInvalidItem(...) maps these to log strings.

Duration scoring:

  • CvCityStrategyAI::ReweightByDuration(...) calls CityStrategyAIHelpers::ReweightByTurnsLeft(weight, turns) on final buildables.
  • CvUnitProductionAI::RecommendUnit(...) also reweights candidate unit weights by turns before selecting a top recommendation.

Flavor sources entering unit weights:

  • City strategy flavor state enters through CvCityStrategyAI::FlavorUpdate() and CvUnitProductionAI::AddFlavorWeights(...).
  • Economic and military strategies propagate city flavor deltas via CvFlavorManager::ChangeCityFlavors(...) when strategies start/end (CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvEconomicAI.cpp, CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvMilitaryAI.cpp, CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvFlavorManager.cpp).
